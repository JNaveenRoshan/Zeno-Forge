# Video script: "Source-to-Pay: how AI agents can help" (about 4 minutes)

Record your screen with your face in a corner (OBS Studio or the Windows Snipping Tool's video mode, both free). Use **Sarvam AI** mode, 2–3 parts, and one browser tab.

| Time | On screen | You say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Campus overview, slowly rotating | "Source-to-Pay: how can AI agents help? I built a working demo on a 3D car-factory campus, with an AI agent at every step from sourcing to payment." |
| 0:15 | Click **Zeno Group HQ → Source-to-Pay**; point at the 8 steps | "Eight steps: parts, suppliers, RFQ, evaluate, negotiate, award, PO to pay, performance. The AI writes and recommends; code enforces the rules; I make the decisions." |
| 0:30 | **1 Parts**: pick the battery, click a supplier tile, add to bid | "I choose the parts to re-source and see how each supplier changes cost, margin and ESG." |
| 0:45 | **2 Suppliers**: set the goal to Greener, show invited suppliers, **Send RFQs** | "Only suppliers that meet our criteria can be invited." |
| 1:00 | **3 RFQ**: RFQ document, quote cards arriving | "A buyer agent writes the RFQ, without revealing our price. Each supplier agent replies with price, lead time, capacity and ESG commitments." |
| 1:30 | **4 Evaluate**: ranking, evaluator recommendation, calendar with red days | "The evaluator agent ranks the quotes. This one is cheaper, but its first delivery comes after our contract ends: 924 cars at risk. That's sourcing measured in cars, not just euros." |
| 2:00 | **5 Negotiate**: Start bidding, lanes moving | "Now the buyer agent negotiates live with each supplier agent. My budget is hidden from them, and their lowest price is hidden from my buyer." |
| 2:30 | **6 Award**: pick offers, the "If you confirm" box, **Confirm** | "I pick the winners and see the effect on margin, ESG and yearly profit before confirming." |
| 2:50 | **7 PO → Pay**: orders running, a red 3-way match failure | "Agents raise the purchase order, confirm, ship, receive and invoice. Here the 3-way match failed: the invoice says €900, but we agreed €859. The AP agent explains why and recommends a fix." Click the fix. |
| 3:20 | **8 Performance** | "Each supplier's score updates from what really happened, and the next round uses it." |
| 3:35 | Back to the campus at night; the AI Data Center glowing | "Every AI action is stored, so it's auditable." |
| 3:45 | Your face | "The AI does the writing, comparing and negotiating. Code enforces the limits. Humans decide. The live 3D demo is in the link; the AI agents are on GitHub to run with your own key." |

**Before recording:** reload the page (Ctrl + F5), keep one tab, and don't switch tabs while the negotiation plays. If no PO fails in step 7, press **↻ Run again**.
